## Future Natural Goals
- "open Chrome, go to Gmail, open the newest email from John and summarize it"
- "open Downloads, find the newest PDF and move it to Documents"
- "watch Antigravity until it finishes and tell me the result"
- "find the browser tab where I was working on the invoice"

> [!quote]
> The user should describe the result naturally. The agent resolves context, chooses the safest method, acts, verifies, and speaks the result.
