# Code of Conduct

We want SHAL Voice PC to be a useful, technical, and welcoming project.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Provide reproducible technical details when reporting problems.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Respect different experience levels.
- Remove credentials and private information from shared logs and screenshots.

Harassment, spam, personal attacks, and deliberate disclosure of private credentials are not acceptable.
